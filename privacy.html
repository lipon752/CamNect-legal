CamNect Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your email address, name and profile picture.
  • Files you upload: course files (such as PDFs, images and documents) and their names and folders.
  • AI chats: the questions you ask the AI assistant and its answers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show your courses.
  • To store and show the files you and your classmates upload.
  • To answer your questions. Your question and the related file text are sent to a third-party AI service to generate a reply.

Who can see your data

Files in a course are visible to that course's members. Your AI chats are visible only to you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. You can delete your files and chats in the app. To delete your account or data, email us.
